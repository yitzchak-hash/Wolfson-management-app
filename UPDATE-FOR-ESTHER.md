# What's new — for Esther

Hi Esther,

We've made some changes to the office system. Nothing you've entered has been lost — all your jobs,
notes, photos and tasks are exactly where you left them. Here's what looks different.

## The name
It's now called the **TzviAir Job Management Platform**. Same system, better name.

## The Job Board is now the first thing you see
When you log in you land straight on the job board instead of a project screen. Wolfson and Netiv are
still there — you switch to them from the buttons at the top.

## It's easier to tell where you are
Each project now has its own colour — Wolfson is gold, Netiv is teal, the Job Board is purple. There's a
coloured stripe down the left side of the screen, so you can always tell at a glance which one you're
looking at, even when you're deep in Settings or Reports.

## Two kinds of settings
There are now two separate settings screens, and this matters:

- **Project settings** (the gear in the left menu) — things that belong to *one* project: its stages, its
  buildings, its contractor sheet. Changing Wolfson's stages no longer affects Netiv.
- **App settings** (the gear at the top right) — things shared by everything: users, contractors,
  language, backups.

This also fixes the problem where the wrong stages were showing up in the wrong project.

## Opening a job
Jobs now open in a **big window in the middle of the screen** instead of the narrow panel that slid in
from the side. Much more room, and it doesn't cover the board.

## The board
- **Zoom in and out** by holding Ctrl and scrolling. Hold the space bar and drag to move around.
- **Job cards look different** — the stage colour is now a thick coloured edge instead of filling the
  whole card, so the name and address stay easy to read.
- Each card shows the **Drive, Zoho and plan buttons** (only when that job has them) and **when it was
  last edited**.
- There's a **toolbar on the right** with labels under each button. You can drag it wherever you like.
- The **Keys** button shows a little list of all the mouse and keyboard shortcuts.
- You can pick from **13 different board looks** — cork board, whiteboard, blueprint and others — in the
  board settings. The current look is still the default.

## Bins: Done, Ready, Archive and Trash
Drag a job into a bin to get it off the board. Click a bin to see what's inside and put anything back
with one click.

**Nothing is ever deleted automatically.** Things in the Trash stay there forever until you choose to
delete them, and you get asked twice before anything really goes.

## The office TV
There's a link in App settings you can open on the TV and put full screen. It shows the live board, and
you can tap between Wolfson, Netiv, the Job Board and a company overview. Tapping a job shows its plan
and latest photos.

**The TV can't change anything** — it can only look. So nobody can accidentally move a job by leaning on
the screen. All changes still happen from a computer.

**If you have notes on the board you'd rather not show on the TV**, each job and note has a small TV
icon. Everything shows by default — click the icon to hide just that one, and it gets a line through it
so you can see at a glance what's hidden.

## The calendar
Much bigger now, and jobs appear as proper cards instead of thin coloured lines. A day with one job shows
it full size.

5 · Questions — the star is my pick

Six things I need from you

1. Where does each apartment's tipus come from?

Wolfson has no tipus list yet, so nothing shows today. Every Wolfson plan already says it in its title block ("טיפוס A").

  • The app reads it off each apartment's plan and fills it in; you fix any it got wrong
  • You type the list in Project settings and set each apartment by hand (or with Bulk Update)

2. What makes an apartment "sold"?

  • It has a family name. No name = Not sold
  • A separate Sold / Not sold switch on each apartment

3. The four filter buttons — one at a time, or together?

  • One at a time, plus All (as drawn). "Sold and upgraded" is not one press
  • Each a switch you can combine

4. The building buttons above the board

  • Say 1 · 2 · 3 too, like the window
  • Keep A1 · A2 · A3 there; only the window says 1 · 2 · 3

5. "The tiny sub-details of each stage that we discussed"

I could not find a list of parts per stage in the past notes. What I drew is the rooms the worker ticked.

  • Show what the worker reported on that stage: the parts he ticked, his note, what was left out
  • You send me the parts for each stage (Drilling: …, Drains: …) and every stage shows its own list

6. With "Current stage" gone, does the tile still name a stage?

  • Yes — the tile keeps the stage being worked on (Drilling), or "Sold" before anything starts
  • The tile shows the next stage instead

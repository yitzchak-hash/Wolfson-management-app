5 · Your answers, locked

The six questions, answered

1. Where each flat's tipus comes from

Found in our own Drive files: on floors 1–13 the four flats go A · B · C1 · C2 in number order, floor 14 and the duplexes are A · B. Drawn flat by flat on the Wolfson Tipus Map. Waiting for your yes on that page.

2. What makes a flat "sold"

A flat with a family name is sold; one without is "Not sold". That is how our Drive already names the folders ("A3/1 - Not Sold").

3. The four filter buttons

★ One at a time, plus All.

4. Building buttons

1 · 2 · 3 everywhere, no "A".

5. A stage's parts

Each stage shows its "includes" list from Settings → Stages. Empty until you fill it; Piping already has Soldering, Connections, Pipes. You will send the rest.

6. The tile still names a stage

★ Yes — the stage being worked on, or "Sold" before anything starts.

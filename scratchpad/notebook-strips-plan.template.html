The rules this would be built to

  • Nothing is hidden — a quiet worker keeps a named strip, never vanishes.
  • A strip takes drops exactly like a full row; it opens as the card passes over it.
  • Squishing is per week and automatic. Strips-vs-tiles is a setting, per notebook.
  • Strips still open the job on a click, still cross off done tasks, still drag between days.

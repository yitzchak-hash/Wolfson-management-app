2.

The card that opens — everything here is live, try it

Put Cohen on Joseph's week?

✕

Saturday never counts. Friday counts only when you say so: set 3 days from Wednesday and an Include Friday? checkbox appears — off, the third day is Sunday; tick it and it's Friday. The checkbox only shows itself when the days actually pass a Friday. Default: off.
Tick Non-consecutive and a second row appears — its own start day and day count (starts at 1). The green line always reads out every day, both stretches together.
And the task now carries ALL of these days, not one date: the worker's schedule shows every one, the calendars plot every one, and it only counts as late after the last one passes.
3.

What lands on the notebook — follows the card above, live

One task, one card per day — the day 1 of 3 pill says it's the same task wearing several faces. Change anything in step 2 and this follows.
When Friday is ticked, the notebook grows a Friday column for that week (its off-day purple). The worker sees his Friday either way.
Afterwards you can still drag any single card to another day — it just moves, no question asked. The question card stays only for a task's last remaining day.

Your four answers, locked in

  1. Saturday never counts. Friday is a per-task checkbox that appears only when the days pass a Friday — default off.
  2. A task carries ALL of its days. The worker's schedule shows every one of them, so does every calendar; it is late only after its last day.
  3. Non-consecutive from the start: a checkbox adds a second stretch (own start day, own count, starting at 1). And dragging one card afterwards just moves that day — no question, except on a task's last remaining day.
  4. Finishing early asks the worker — big simple words in his own language, after photos + Close task. Yes crosses the remaining days off; done days wear the strike-through line as the record.

All four are built exactly as locked. Drag a job onto the notebook on the live site and the new card is what opens.

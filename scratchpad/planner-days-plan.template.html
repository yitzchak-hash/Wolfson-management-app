★

Four things for you two to decide

  1. Do the days skip Friday and Saturday? The notebook shows Sunday–Thursday, so we'd count only working days: 3 days starting Thursday = Thu, Sun, Mon. Our recommendation: yes, skip them.
  2. Which date does the task itself carry? We'd make its due date the LAST day — it's late only once all its days have passed. Recommendation: last day.
  3. Dragging one day of a multi-day task: we'd have it just move, with no question card — the drop question stays only for a task's final remaining day (where moving off means leaving the notebook). Recommendation: just move.
  4. Finishing early: when the task is closed on day 2 of 3, the leftover day's card disappears with the rest. Recommendation: yes, it goes.

Say yes (or change any of the four) and we build it exactly as drawn. The week order flip you asked about — older weeks on top, big AUG, bigger eyes, the little up/down arrows — is already done and live; it didn't need a page.

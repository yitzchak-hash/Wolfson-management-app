The Done Line

You said: on the page where it starts a list of his done tasks, it says Done and then the tasks. That should be collapsible with a tiny little arrow at the edge, and little tiny filter buttons that are very, very clean and subtle on that line.

Three ways to do it, drawn with the phone's own cards. Every phone below works — press the arrow, press the filters. All names are made up. Nothing here is built yet.

A

The words sit on the line itself

my pick — nothing new on the screen, just quiet words

  1. 1The line reads ✓ Done · 24 as today, then three grey words: Latest · Today · This week. The chosen one turns navy and underlined. No boxes, no colours — on a phone they read as part of the line.
  2. 2The tiny arrow sits at the line's far edge. Press it and the done list folds away to just the line; press again and it opens. The phone remembers which way he left it.
  3. 3Latest is the newest closed first — the job he closed ten minutes ago is on top. Today and This week keep only what he closed then, with the count on the line changing to match.

try it → press "Today", then the arrow

B

Tiny chips under the line

easier to hit with a thumb, a bit more on screen

  1. 4Five small white chips in one row under the line: Latest · Oldest · Today · This week · By building. Bigger targets than A, at the cost of one more thin row.
  2. 5By building groups the done jobs under small grey headings — A1, A2, A3 — for a worker who did a whole floor in one tower.
C

One small "Latest ▾" button

the quietest — one word, the rest in a menu

  1. 6Only one small button on the line, saying what the list is sorted by. Pressing it opens a short menu with every choice: Latest, Oldest, Today, This week, Last week, By building, By workspace.
  2. 7Nothing to see until he wants it — but every choice takes two presses instead of one.
